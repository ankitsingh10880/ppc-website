About the club

A student-run club at FMS for anyone who wants to understand how policy shapes business.

Who we are

The Public Policy Club is currently an interest-based club at the Faculty of Management Studies, University of Delhi, founded in February 2025. It brings together students who want to understand how laws, regulation and public spending shape the markets they will work in.

We read and summarise policy, debate it, and invite the people who make and advise on it to speak at FMS. Our work is guided by faculty advisors and draws on the FMS alumni network in government and policy.

What we set out to do

  • Awareness

    Make policy issues part of everyday conversation in the FMS community.

  • Discussion

    Encourage debate on public policy, especially where it meets business.

  • Exposure

    Bring alumni and policy practitioners to campus to talk about their work.

  • Community

    Build a network of peers who want to understand how policy really works.

  • Projects

    Support live projects, case competitions and governance consulting, with other FMS clubs.

  • Connections

    Build links with public policy institutes and forums in Delhi.

How we do it

Through the year the club runs a flagship panel on Constitution Day (26 November), an event for students across Delhi University during Fiesta, an induction for each new batch, and regular smaller sessions.

  • Guest lectures
  • Policy workshops
  • Mock parliament debates
  • Monthly roundtables
  • Union Budget discussions
  • Networking events
