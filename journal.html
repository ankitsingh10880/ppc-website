PPC Journal

Business-focused summaries of the policy developments every manager should know.

Volume 1, 2025

The first edition of the club's journal, written for final-year students preparing for placements. Each entry explains what a policy means, why it matters to companies, and gives real examples. With an introductory address by Dr. Aasheerwad Dwivedi, Assistant Professor of Economics, FMS.

Read Volume 1 (PDF, 6.1 MB, opens in a new tab) Download

In this volume

  1. International Relations

    Multipolarity, geo-economics, strategic autonomy, supply-chain geopolitics and the Indo-Pacific.

  2. Economy

    AI and employment, GST rationalisation, logistics reform and rupee internationalisation.

  3. Science & Tech

    Health AI, data protection, quantum and deep-tech missions, and space-sector FDI.

  4. Ethics

    Ethical values, leadership, conflicts of interest, media and technology ethics.

  5. Sustainability

    Climate action, carbon markets, greenwashing, and plastic and e-waste rules.
